## Scope

This reproduction targets claims from the live challenge record for Hibiki-Zero (Simultaneous Speech-to-Speech Translation Without Aligned Data):

1. Hibiki-Zero trains simultaneous speech translation from sentence-level aligned speech data rather than word-level aligned interpretation data (Section 3).
2. Hibiki-Zero casts latency-quality optimization as reinforcement learning with GRPO over multistream speech/text outputs (Section 3.3).
3. The final Hibiki-Zero architecture is a 3B-parameter decoder-only multistream model built on RQ-Transformer audio token modeling (Section 4.1).
4. On Europarl-ST and Audio-NTREX-4L, Hibiki-Zero reports better long-form ASR BLEU, speaker similarity, and latency than Seamless, and improves over Hibiki on French short-form ASR BLEU (Table 1).
5. Human MOS evaluations rate Hibiki-Zero higher than Seamless on audio quality, speaker similarity, and speech naturalness across evaluated input languages (Table 2).
6. The model adapts to Italian-to-English simultaneous translation using 850 hours of fine-tuning data and reports Seamless-level quality/latency with better speaker similarity after RL (Table 3).

The evidence will include a deterministic CPU verification of model architecture semantics, GRPO multistream reward computation, sentence-level alignment loss formulation, and benchmark evaluation harness logic. Full 3B 850-hour GPU training and human MOS evaluations are out of scope for CPU execution and will be documented as limitations.
