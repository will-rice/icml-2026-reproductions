## Target Claims

1. **OeMDM NELBO Decomposition (Proposition 3.2)**: OeMDM defines a generalized masked-diffusion NELBO that decomposes into a reconstruction term and a velocity-mismatch term for order-aware generation processes.
2. **Autoregressive Recovery (Proposition 3.3)**: OeMDM recovers autoregressive left-to-right modeling as a special case under a suitable scheduler.
3. **LoMDM Single-Objective Joint Training (Section 4.1)**: LoMDM jointly trains the diffusion backbone and learnable order schedulers from scratch with a single objective rather than post-training an unmasking sampler.
