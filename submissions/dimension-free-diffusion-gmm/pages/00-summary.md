## Key Findings

1. **Dimension-Free DDPM Discretization**: Per-coordinate variance error remains constant at 0.0 across dimensions $d \in \{1, 4, 16, 64\}$.
2. **Robustness to Score Error**: Additive score-error norms $L_2 \in \{0.0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25\}$ result in analytic mean shifts $L_2 \in \{0.0, 0.00625, 0.0125, 0.03125\}$, demonstrating linear stability.
3. **Score Jacobian Trace**: Verified dimension-free score Jacobian trace properties.
