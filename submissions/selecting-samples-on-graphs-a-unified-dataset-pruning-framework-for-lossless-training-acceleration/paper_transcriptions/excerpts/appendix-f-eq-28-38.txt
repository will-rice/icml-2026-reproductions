f(S*) ≤ f(S_t) + Σ_{x ∈ S* \ S_t} Δ(x | S_t)
      ≤ f(S_t) + (b − t) max_{x ∈ T \ S_t} Δ(x | S_t).  (28)

max_x Δ(x | S_t) = f(S_{t+1}) − f(S_t).  (29)

f(S*) − f(S_t) ≤ (b − t)(f(S_{t+1}) − f(S_t)).  (30)

f(S*) − f(S_{t+1})
= (f(S*) − f(S_t)) − (f(S_{t+1}) − f(S_t)).  (31)

f(S*) − f(S_{t+1})
≤ (f(S*) − f(S_t)) − (f(S*) − f(S_t))/(b − t)
= (1 − 1/(b − t))(f(S*) − f(S_t)).  (32)

Δ_{t+1} ≤ (1 − 1/(b − t)) Δ_t,  t = 0, 1, ..., b − 1.  (33)

Δ_b ≤ Π_{t=0}^{b−1}(1 − 1/(b − t)) Δ_0
    = Π_{k=1}^{b}(1 − 1/k) Δ_0.  (34)

Π_{k=1}^{b}(1 − 1/k) ≤ (1 − 1/b)^b.  (35)

(1 − 1/b)^b = e^{b ln(1 − 1/b)} ≤ e^{b·(−1/b)} = 1/e.  (36)

f(S*) − f(S_greedy) = Δ_b ≤ (1/e) f(S*).  (37)

f(S_greedy) ≥ (1 − 1/e) f(S*).  (38)
